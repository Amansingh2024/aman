Specialized Divisions

8 Integrated Clinical Disciplines Under One Roof

From routine screening to advanced molecular sequencing, our Budh Marg central facility houses specialized pathology domains led by experienced medical pathologists and senior technologists.

Division 01

Biochemistry

High-throughput automated clinical chemistry analyzers handling metabolic panels, liver & kidney assays, electrolyte ion-selective electrode (ISE) profiling, and enzymatic kinetic tests.

Auto-Analyzers Same Day
Division 02 • AIIMS EQAP

Hematology

Automated 5-part differential cell counters, peripheral blood smear cytology by senior pathologists, HPLC hemoglobin variant analysis (Beta Thalassemia screening), and PT/INR coagulation assays.

AIIMS EQAP Same Day
Division 03

Immunology & Serology

Chemiluminescence Immunoassays (CLIA) and ELISA platforms for automated thyroid panels, Vitamin D3, Vitamin B12, fertility hormones, cardiac troponin, and viral markers (HIV, HBV, HCV).

CLIA Platforms Same Day
Division 04

Microbiology

Automated blood culture monitoring, precise minimum inhibitory concentration (MIC) antibiotic sensitivity panels, fungal investigations, AFB tuberculosis staining, and gram smear evaluations.

Automated MIC 48-72 Hours
Division 05

Clinical Pathology

Digital urine sediment analysis, automated routine urinalysis with protein-creatinine ratio (ACR), WHO-standard semen analysis, stool occult blood, and body fluid microscopic examinations.

Microscopic Flow Same Day
Division 06

Histopathology

Comprehensive surgical tissue biopsy processing, grossing, automatic vacuum tissue processor, microtomy, routine Hematoxylin & Eosin (H&E) staining, and specialized oncopathology reporting.

Biopsy Sectioning 3-5 Days

Cytopathology

Fine Needle Aspiration Cytology (FNAC) by experienced pathologists, liquid-based cervical Pap smear cytology, and exfoliative fluid cytology for early pre-cancerous and malignancy detection.

FNAC & Pap Smear 24-48 Hours

Facility Excellence

Advanced Diagnostic Architecture & Safety Systems

Every sample passing through Sen Diagnostics is safeguarded by institutional protocols developed over six decades of medical leadership.

100% Barcoding & LIMS

Pioneered in 2001 as the first laboratory in India to barcode every tube at patient collection. Our proprietary LIMS interfaces directly with automated analyzers, eliminating human transcription error.

Zero-Mixup Specimen Security

Unbroken Cold Chain Transport

All specimens collected from home visits or peripheral clinics are transported in calibrated thermo-insulated cool boxes with frozen gel packs. Monitored strictly at 2°C to 8°C until loading onto instruments.

Calibrated Temperature Logs

Dual-Tier Quality Control (QC)

Every analytical run begins with multi-level internal controls (Westgard rules). Concurrently benchmarked against external proficiency schemes including AIIMS New Delhi and CMC Vellore.
