Our Foundation & History

Laboratory Services Closer to You in Patna, Bihar

Sen Diagnostics Private Limited is a family-owned pathology laboratory established in Patna in 1965 by Dr. Dilip Sen. Across three generations of pathologists, we have continued to develop our laboratory services through advances in diagnostic technology, quality systems, and rigorous medical oversight.

Through our central laboratory on Budh Marg, our expanding network of 15+ collection points, and doorstep home sample collection services, we provide patients, clinicians, and hospitals access to a comprehensive spectrum of laboratory investigations across Bihar.

Our Core Vision

"Advancing Laboratory Medicine in Bihar — building upon six decades of clinical trust by continually adopting automated platforms, adhering to global quality standards, and maintaining uncompromised patient diagnostic safety."

Six Decades of Innovation

Sen Diagnostics Historical Timeline Tree

Scroll down to watch milestones emerge along our diagnostic tree. Click any milestone card to float it in 3D and inspect archival records.

1965 Click to Float 3D

Humble Beginnings in Patna

Dr. Dilip Sen founded Sen Diagnostics laboratory in Patna, beginning from a modest 8x8 consulting room with a singular dedication to medical truth.

Historical Impact: Established ethical, physician-led pathology in Bihar when diagnostic infrastructure was non-existent in the private sector.
1966 Click to Float 3D

Enzymology & Steroid Assays

First laboratory in the region to introduce clinical bacteriology, paper chromatography, and quantitative enzyme & steroid assays outside a medical college hospital.

Historical Impact: Eliminated the requirement for patients in Bihar to send critical hormone and metabolic samples to Kolkata or New Delhi.
1969 Click to Float 3D

First Private Licensed Blood Bank

Commissioned the first private licensed blood bank in the State of Bihar, setting strict cross-matching and donor safety benchmarks.

Historical Impact: Saved thousands of acute trauma, surgical, and postpartum hemorrhage patients during critical clinical emergencies in Patna.
1974 Click to Float 3D

Hematology Automation (Coulter Counter)

Pioneered automated cell counting in Eastern India by importing the world-renowned Coulter counter for high-precision complete blood counts.

Historical Impact: Shifted hematology from manual hemocytometer chambers to impedance aperture precision, slashing turnaround times from hours to minutes.
1980 Click to Float 3D

BARC Radioisotope Approval

Recognized as the first non-government radioisotope immunoassay laboratory authorized by the Bhabha Atomic Research Centre (BARC) for RIA hormone quantitation.

Historical Impact: Enabled ultra-sensitive detection of sub-nanogram thyroid, reproductive, and adrenal hormones with nuclear regulatory compliance.
1984 Click to Float 3D

First Custom LIMS by Dr. Sandeep Sen

Dr. Sandeep Sen personally programmed an in-house Laboratory Information Management System (LIMS) on an IBM PC, heralding the digital pathology era in Bihar.

Historical Impact: Complete digital archival of patient reports, quality control statistics, and automated analytical range validation.
1994 Click to Float 3D

Molecular PCR Diagnostics

Established the first non-government Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR) molecular genetics and infectious disease lab in Eastern India.

Historical Impact: Pioneered DNA amplification for tuberculosis (Mycobacterium TB), Hepatitis B viral DNA, and oncological translocations.
2001 Click to Float 3D

First in India to Barcode Specimens

Became the first diagnostic laboratory in all of India to implement universal barcode identification right at patient phlebotomy.

Historical Impact: Zero sample mix-ups, bidirectional analyzer interfacing, and 100% infallible traceability from vein to validated report.
2015 Click to Float 3D

WHO GeneXpert TB Partnership

Deployed WHO-approved GeneXpert cartridge testing for rapid multidrug-resistant tuberculosis (MDR-TB) in partnership with the Clinton Health Access Initiative.

Historical Impact: Same-day detection of Mycobacterium tuberculosis and Rifampicin resistance mutations in vulnerable communities.
2020 Click to Float 3D

ICMR COVID-19 RT-PCR Lab

Appointed as the first private NABL and ICMR approved COVID-19 RT-PCR testing center in Bihar during the global pandemic emergency.

Historical Impact: Round-the-clock testing infrastructure serving Patna citizens, healthcare workers, and critical emergency hospital admissions.
2024 & Beyond Click to Float 3D

Cloud-Native LIMS & Automated Microbiology

Transitioned to cloud-native diagnostic LIMS, 15+ doorstep phlebotomy centers in Patna, and automated MIC antibiotic susceptibility testing.

Historical Impact: Instant WhatsApp encrypted report dispatch, real-time QC tracking with AIIMS New Delhi EQAP, and next-generation diagnostic robotics.
