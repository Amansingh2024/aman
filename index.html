Six Decades of Innovation Since 1965

Sen Diagnostics Timeline & Care Tree

Scroll down to watch milestones emerge along our tree. Click any milestone card to float it in 3D and explore regional diagnostic breakthroughs.

1965 Click to Float 3D

Founded by Dr. Dilip Sen

Dr. Dilip Sen began Sen Diagnostics in Patna from an 8x8 consulting room with an unwavering pursuit of pathology accuracy.

Key Achievement: Introduced ethical private pathology practice to Bihar, setting regional standards for clinical accuracy.
1969 Click to Float 3D

First Private Blood Bank in Bihar

Established Bihar's first licensed private blood banking facility, providing emergency cross-matching for critical surgeries.

Key Achievement: Transformed emergency medicine in Patna by ensuring 24/7 cross-matched safe blood availability.
1974 Click to Float 3D

First Coulter Counter in Region

First in the region to introduce automated hematology testing with the famous Coulter counter, cutting turnaround time dramatically.

Key Achievement: Replaced slow manual chamber counts with electronic precision aperture impedance.
1984 Click to Float 3D

First In-House LIMS on IBM PC

Dr. Sandeep Sen custom-programmed a proprietary Laboratory Information Management System (LIMS) running on an IBM personal computer.

Key Achievement: Automated reporting, statistical quality control tracking, and permanent digital patient record keeping.
2001 Click to Float 3D

First in India to Barcode Every Tube

Pioneered universal sample barcoding across the country, ensuring complete elimination of patient mix-up errors.

Key Achievement: Direct bidirectional instrument interface; became a national benchmark for pre-analytical safety.
2020 Click to Float 3D

First ICMR COVID-19 RT-PCR Lab in Bihar

Approved by ICMR & NABL as the state's first private molecular laboratory for critical COVID-19 pandemic testing.

Key Achievement: Conducted round-the-clock testing to protect hospital admissions and frontline medical staff in Bihar.
2024 & Beyond Click to Float 3D

Cloud-Native Diagnostics & Automated MIC

15+ collection centers across Patna, instant WhatsApp report dispatch, and automated microbiology with AIIMS EQAP oversight.

Key Achievement: Seamless digital patient portal with QR verification and unbroken specimen cold-chain logistics.
