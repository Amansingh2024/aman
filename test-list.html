Clinical Instructions

Pre-Test Preparation Guidelines

Ensuring precise analytical results starts with proper specimen preparation. Follow these medical recommendations prior to blood or urine sample collection.

Fasting Protocols

Tests such as Fasting Blood Glucose (FBS), Lipid Profile, and HOMA-IR require an overnight 10 to 12-hour fast.

  • Only plain water is permitted during fasting.
  • Avoid tea, coffee, milk, and chewables.
  • Do not exceed 14 hours of prolonged starvation.

Medication Timings

Consult your physician before pausing regular prescription drugs prior to diagnostics.

  • Thyroid Tests: Take Thyroxine tablets after giving your morning blood sample.
  • Diabetes Meds: Take with post-meal breakfast.
  • Inform the phlebotomist about blood thinners (Aspirin, Warfarin).

Urine & Culture Samples

Accurate microbiological and urinalysis parameters require a clean-catch midstream technique.

  • First morning urine specimen is preferred.
  • Discard initial 15-20 ml stream and catch midstream directly into sterile container.
  • Collect prior to starting any antibiotic course.
